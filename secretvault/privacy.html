Privacy Policy - The Secret Vault

Developer: Owais Studios · App: The Secret Vault (Android, package com.owaisstudios.secretvault) · Effective date: 1 October 2026 (version 1.0.0 and later) · Website: owaisfirpo.github.io

In short:
  • The game works offline. Your progress is saved on your own device.
  • If you use the online leaderboards, the game creates an anonymous player ID (no sign-up, no email, no password) and uploads your codename, avatar, level and scores so that other players can see the boards. See section 3.
  • The game shows optional reward videos from Google AdMob. Google collects device and ad data to show them, with your consent where the law requires it. See section 4.
  • Share and Follow buttons only open your phone's own apps (for example WhatsApp, Facebook or the browser) with a ready text or link; the game itself sends nothing.
  • There is no sign-in and no in-app purchase in this version. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

The Secret Vault is a mystery quiz and puzzle game made by Owais Studios, a small independent game developer. This policy explains which information the game uses, why, and what you can do about it.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves files in its private app storage on your phone:

  • Your progress and settings: missions, stars, scores, coins, hint tokens, vault keys, achievements, badges, streak, cosmetic items, your agent codename and avatar, and settings such as sound volume, vibration and accessibility options. A small check value helps the game notice if the file was edited by hand.
  • A copy made when you reset your progress, so nothing is lost by accident.
  • Your online identity: the anonymous player ID and the sign-in tokens the leaderboards need.
  • Scores waiting for upload, so a result made offline can be sent later.
  • Ad limits: how many reward videos you watched today and when, so the daily limits and waiting times work.

We cannot see these files. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

3. Online leaderboards (Google Firebase)

To run fair Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Global leaderboards, the game uses Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a service of Google that stores data for us.

What is sent:

  • An anonymous player ID created by Firebase the first time the game goes online. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or Google account.
  • Your public agent profile: codename, avatar, avatar frame, agent level, XP, achievement points and number of badges.
  • Your leaderboard entries: the board and period (for example "Daily, 1 October 2026"), your score, how long the run took, how many hints you used, the number of weekly attempts, the game version and content version, and the time the entry was saved.

Why: to show the leaderboards, to rank you, to give end-of-week rewards by rank, and to keep the boards fair (the server rejects impossible scores and lets every player change only their own entries).

Who can see it: other players of The Secret Vault see your codename, avatar, frame, level, XP, achievement points and score on the boards. The other fields are stored with your entry so the game can check and rank results. Google stores the data for us on its servers in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. Google's servers also see your device's IP address when the game connects, and Firebase uses it for security and abuse protection under Google's own policies.

What is not sent: no real name, no email, no phone number, no contacts, no location, and no device serial numbers or advertising ID from our own code.

Your codename: the game suggests a codename such as "Agent Falcon-2941". You can change it (at most once every 24 hours). Please do not use your real name or any personal information: codenames are public. The game blocks offensive words, reserved names and contact handles in codenames. There is no chat and no private messaging.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, to show rewarded videos only:

  • A video plays only when you tap a button that offers a reward (for example a free hint, bonus coins, an extra life or extra time). Nothing is lost if you never watch one.
  • There are no banner ads, no full-screen ads between missions, and no ads inside Daily Dossier or Weekly Operation runs or in the first training missions.
  • Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (AdMob maximum ad content rating "PG").

To show, measure and (with your consent where required) personalise ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google: device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (or an app set ID), your IP address (which Google uses to estimate a coarse, city-level location), device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), information about the ads you see and tap, and crash and performance diagnostics of the SDK. This data is sent encrypted. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under its own policies. Owais Studios only receives anonymous totals (for example how many ads were shown).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, How Google uses advertising data, AdMob and user privacy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads. If you refuse, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings > Ad privacy (this row appears where it is required). You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's settings.

US state privacy laws: Owais Studios does not sell personal information for money. Where applicable, Google's restricted data processing and your device's advertising settings let you limit personalised ads.

5. Sharing and links

  • Share (after a mission, a vault, a Daily Dossier, a Weekly Operation, or on your profile) builds a short text, for example your result and a link to the game's Play Store page. WhatsApp and Facebook open those apps or their web pages with the text ready; COPY TEXT puts it on your phone's clipboard. Nothing is posted unless you send it yourself, and the game does not receive anything back.
  • Follow Owais Studios (Settings) and the Privacy policy row open our Facebook page or website in your browser or the Facebook app.
  • Those apps and websites handle your data under their own privacy policies.

6. Purchases

This version has no real-money purchases. Coins, hint tokens and vault keys are earned by playing.

7. What we do not collect

The game does not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location or health data. It does not request the location permission. It has no chat, no friends list and no sign-in. Owais Studios runs no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, stores only the leaderboard data described in section 3, and does not sell your personal information.

8. Permissions

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): online leaderboards, reward videos and Google's consent form. The game itself works offline; leaderboard scores are sent later, and without internet no videos are offered.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK): lets the ad SDK read the Android Advertising ID. You can reset or delete this ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): short vibration feedback; you can switch it off in the game's Settings.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may add a few technical permissions of its own (for example to keep background work running briefly); the game does not use them for anything else.

9. Children

The Secret Vault is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Its content is family friendly (no violence, no bad language). Ad requests are marked as not child-directed and ads are limited to general-audience content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please contact us and we will delete their leaderboard data.

10. Your choices

  • Reset progress (Settings > Reset progress) erases missions, coins, stars, streak and achievements on your device. Settings stay. It does not remove or lower your online leaderboard records (so reset cannot be used to replay ranks), and your anonymous player ID stays the same.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes everything the game stored on your device. Leaderboard entries already uploaded stay on the boards until they are deleted as described below.
  • Ads: change your consent in Settings > Ad privacy, and reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your leaderboard data: email owais.ahmed171986@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", your codename and roughly when you last played. We will delete your public profile and all your leaderboard entries within 30 days and confirm by email. If data protection laws give you further rights (for example access or correction), write to the same address.

11. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Online leaderboard entries and public profiles are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until we remove old boards. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies.

12. Security

The game's internet traffic (Firebase, Google AdMob and Google's consent form) uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Our Firebase security rules allow each player to write only their own entries and reject impossible scores. Your save file is protected by the normal security of your Android device.

13. Changes to this policy

If a new version of the game handles data differently (for example in-app purchases), we will update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above. The newest version of this policy will always be available at this address.

14. Contact
